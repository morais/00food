# 00Food for iOS

**Your food log. Your AI agent.** The sign-in screen introduces the bring-your-own-agent workflow. After profile setup, accounts without an agent see a prominent Connect your agent card. The same guide is available in Settings, recommends ChatGPT Work, and provides copyable MCP connection and event-monitoring instructions. It shows authorized connections separately from their currently unexpired event subscriptions. New-food estimates remain subject to user review; manual logging remains available. See [agent setup](../docs/agent-setup.md).

SwiftUI app for iPhone, built with Xcode 27 and XcodeGen. The app and widget target iPhone only (`TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY = 1`); Mac Catalyst, Mac compatibility destinations, and Apple Vision compatibility destinations are disabled. TestFlight groups also disable Apple silicon Mac and Apple Vision availability. Native iPad support and iPad App Store screenshots are removed; iOS may still permit installation in iPhone compatibility mode on iPad. The main screen shows today's rough balance, pending agent estimates, and today's entries. Home Screen quick actions open Log food or open Log food directly into the camera. Log food prioritizes saved foods for one-tap reuse and has no built-in starter list. Unfamiliar foods can be saved with a manual estimate or sent with a description, a photo, or both to a remote MCP agent for review. The new-food form keeps its wrapping description field (growing up to six lines) and attached-photo preview in one section, with Photos and Camera side by side. Manual entry is a separate section. The description and photo are submitted atomically under the same pending-food ID so the agent can consider both.

After the first successful sign-in and sync, the app opens from an on-device snapshot without a connection. Saved foods, manual foods, logs, deletions, profile edits, and new estimate requests (including their photos) are saved locally and replayed in order when the connection returns. The original meal date and time are preserved. A status strip shows queued changes and sync errors. Sign-out waits until queued changes have synced, so it cannot silently discard them. Initial Apple sign-in, agent estimation and review, account deletion, and MCP connection management require a connection. Apple Health reads work on device; the widget uses the latest local app snapshot.

```sh
cp project.yml.sample project.yml
# Set your Apple team, bundle ID, and FoodServerBaseURL in project.yml.
xcodegen generate --spec project.yml

```

The app App ID needs Sign in with Apple, HealthKit, and App Groups. Register a widget extension App ID and assign the same App Group to both App IDs. It reads `activeEnergyBurned`, `basalEnergyBurned`, `bodyMass`, `bodyFatPercentage`, and `dietaryWater` from Apple Health. The eight water cups beneath Log Food reflect today's Health total; each tap writes another 250 mL sample. A new day clears the water display, and a HealthKit no-data result shows zero cups without an error. Your details & Health can write a manual weight sample to Health and save the value to the 00Food profile. The daily budget uses the average resting energy from at least five of the last seven completed days; it falls back to the details-based estimate when that history is unavailable. Your details & Health shows both resting-energy values, with their explanation and difference behind info buttons. New logs can be exported as `dietaryEnergyConsumed` after the user enables writing on the Developer page opened by tapping the version in Settings. Exports are keyed by account and log ID; deleting a log deletes its matching Health sample. Earlier logs are not backfilled. Health energy and historical measurements stay on device by default. If Daily feedback is enabled in Settings, the app automatically uploads up to seven completed days of available Health daily aggregates to the private 00Food account, with an offline queue, on the next open or after midnight while active. With daily feedback enabled, it also requests an on-device background refresh after 00:15 local time through BGAppRefreshTask. iOS chooses the actual execution time. Locked Health data or failed sync defers the request and schedules a retry at least an hour later; completed runs schedule the next local 00:15, accounting for timezone and daylight-saving changes. Background refresh submits the same MCP event as foreground requests, and the connected agent still produces the review. No server midnight scheduler is used. Each past day in the food log shows its review or a request action when missing, even when automatic feedback is off. Review requests cover the last 90 days since account creation and save offline first. A food-only MCP connection does not gain Health access on reconnect alone. Rescan the MCP server and invoke the daily feedback tools to request `daily:read` and `daily:write`; Settings shows whether both permissions were granted. Your details automatically uses the latest readable recorded weight and offers manual weight logging instead of an editable weight field. Saving details stores that weight in the Cloudflare profile. The fallback resting estimate also uses the latest readable weight, while an available Health resting-energy average takes precedence. Progress and plans opens from the toolbar beside Settings. It shows only the saved plan by default; Other plans expands maintain (0), gentle (300), steady (450), and faster (600 kcal/day) alternatives for comparison and switching. A calorie history chart compares food with the current food target plus each day's Health active energy over 30 or 90 days. Each day uses a stacked bar: orange food within the allowance, green remaining allowance above food, or red excess above the allowance. Bar height is the greater of food and allowance, so excess is not counted twice. Today uses the same styling as every other day. It uses the current target for earlier dates because past plan changes and resting energy are not tracked. The other charts run over six calendar months, with illustrative weight lines and actual weight/body-fat history since account creation. The weight axis starts at the weight corresponding to adult BMI 18.5; a shaded band reaches BMI 24.9, and a date appears when an estimate enters that band. The body-fat chart keeps ACE's 25% male or 32% female obesity boundary and adds other ACE category boundaries only when the projected line crosses them; neutral shows no category lines. Its dotted illustration assumes all projected weight loss comes from fat, with lean mass unchanged. Weight-chart months are abbreviated and the axis uses 5 kg ticks. Each plan card has its own chart info button; ACE thresholds and forecast dates stay in the legend without duplicate inline labels. BMI is a screening measure, and the forecasts are directional. The allowance adds Health active energy once; exercise minutes are not converted to calories. The target in `project.yml.sample` uses example identifiers; the real project file is ignored.

The balance card has no heading. Tap its calorie figure to reveal the resting estimate and calorie-gap calculation; it starts closed. The medium Home Screen widget shows the same balance, eaten and active calories, and two-point day pace line, plus a Log button. Control Center offers Log food (fork and knife) and Photograph food (plate-style fork and knife) controls; the latter opens the camera in the log form. Log Food has a fixed Your foods / New food switch: repeat logging and search stay in the first tab; agent estimation and manual calories stay in the second. A failed search can carry its text into New food, and the camera shortcut selects New food before opening the camera. The water row uses eight drinking-glass outlines that fill blue as Health water is recorded. The Food estimates entry sits directly below Log food. Send clarification uses a full-width prominent button with an empty-input disabled state. Today separates counts for estimates awaiting the agent, estimates ready to review, and requests saved offline; each food estimate has a matching status badge. The widget holds a token-free local snapshot and refreshes when the app refreshes or the log changes, so Health active energy may be stale while 00Food has not run. The home review card is titled Yesterday and selects that day specifically. The home screen's food log date control and arrows show previous days. Their summary keeps logged calories, Apple Health water in 250 mL glass equivalents, and fruit/vegetable portions (capped at five) on one line. Missing Health access shows a dash, while a day with no water samples shows zero. Swipe an entry to delete it. The balance card shows two points on one line: the share of the active day elapsed and the share of the current calorie allowance eaten. The active-day window defaults to 7 a.m.–11 p.m. and can be changed in Your details & Health, with its explanation below the time fields. The food percentage uses the base target plus Apple Health active energy earned so far, so it may decrease as more activity is recorded. Its marker turns yellow when the allowance-used share exceeds the active-day share. The two percentages use a slightly larger, Dynamic Type-scaled font. The chart is a comparison, not a prescription to eat at a steady rate. Pending estimates are excluded from food used. The snapshot currently supplies the last 90 days of logs. Birth year is optional in Your details; when provided, the app uses approximate age in the resting estimate, otherwise it uses age 35 as a reference. The app asks for birth year directly and does not request Health date of birth.

For a simulator compile without signing:

```sh
xcodegen generate --spec project.yml.sample
xcodebuild -project ZeroZeroFood.xcodeproj -scheme ZeroZeroFood \
  -configuration Debug -destination 'generic/platform=iOS Simulator' \
  -derivedDataPath "$PWD/DerivedData" CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO build
```

For TestFlight, create the app record in App Store Connect, use separate App Store distribution provisioning profiles for the app and widget extension with their required capabilities, archive the release scheme, export an IPA, and upload it to App Store Connect. Set `MARKETING_VERSION` to `1.0` and `CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION` to the Lisbon local build timestamp (`TZ=Europe/Lisbon date +%Y%m%d%H%M`) before archiving, as in 00Todo. Once Apple finishes processing, `scripts/assign-testflight.py --app-id APP_ID --group-name Internal --build BUILD_NUMBER` creates or finds the app's internal group, assigns the build, and verifies the relationship. Add existing internal testers on the group's Testers page in App Store Connect. Set `ASC_KEY_PATH` to the App Store Connect API key when `~/.appstoreconnect/private_keys` contains more than one key; the Sign in with Apple key is separate.
